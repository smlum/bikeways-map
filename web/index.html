Explore the CCND, check it against OpenStreetMap, log issues.

    About

    An (unofficial) tool for exploring Statistics Canada's Canadian Cycling Network Database. The CCND is made up of municipal open data, classified using Can-BICS.

    The tool also displays CHATR Lab's Can-BICS OpenStreetMap network for comparison, labelled as OSM.

    Using it

    • Pick a city with the search box or by clicking it on the map.
    • CCND and OSM show each network by Can-BICS class.
    • Compare shows where they match or differ.

    Add an issue

    • Click a segment for its details and to add an issue.

    Data

    • CCND, Statistics Canada: compiled Nov 2023–Mar 2024 from 75 municipal open datasets published 2018–2024. Released under the Open Government Licence – Canada. Municipal licences listed in the CCND download.
    • Can-BICS OpenStreetMap, CHATR Lab: classified from OpenStreetMap as of 15 Jan 2024. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
    • Classes follow the Can-BICS framework (see CHATR Lab's visual guide).
    • Boundaries: 2021 census subdivisions, Statistics Canada.
